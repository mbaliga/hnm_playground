A workbench for designing haptic effects and the sounds that go with them. Build an
effect on a timeline, feel it on the device in your hand, and export it into a real
app.

Two things at once, on purpose: a playground for exploring how a haptic feels, and
a developer tool that produces something you can ship.

ONE EFFECT, EVERY BACKEND
Audio sounds the same everywhere. Haptics do not: every platform has a different
actuator, and most desktops have none at all. So an effect here is not written
against a particular vibrator. It is a definition of events on a timeline, each
with an intensity and a sharpness plus animatable curves, and each playback backend
interprets that same definition with whatever hardware it has.

Design the effect once. The phone's actuator, a controller's voice coil, or plain
audio each render it their own way.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Place events on a timeline and shape them with breakpoint curves.
• Couple a haptic to its audio so the two are designed as one thing.
• Feel it immediately on the device you are holding.
• Save and load effects in a plain, readable format.
• Export for use in your own app.

BUILT AS A TOOL, NOT A TOY
The effect definition is the keystone, and it is deliberately portable. Files you
make here are readable outside this app and are not locked to it.

QUIET BY DESIGN
• No ads, no accounts, no analytics, no tracking.
• No network permission at all. Your effects never leave your device.

Haptics on a device with a weak or absent actuator will feel weak or absent. That
is the hardware, not the app, and the workbench shows you which backend is
actually running.
