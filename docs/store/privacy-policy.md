# Privacy Policy — Haptics Workbench

> **This is the working copy, not the hosted one.** The URL Play Console points at
> is **https://asystemofcells.com/haptics/privacy**. Keep the two in sync by hand.

**Last updated: 29 August 2026**

Haptics Workbench is a haptic and audio design tool for Android (package
`dev.hnm.workbench.android`), made by A System of Cells. This policy is short
because the app does almost nothing with data.

## The short version

Haptics Workbench has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.
**The app has no internet permission at all.** Nothing can leave your device, and
nothing does.

## What the app collects

**Nothing.** We operate no servers and receive nothing.

## What is stored, and where

The effects you design, and your settings, are stored on your device. Effects you
save are ordinary files in a plain, readable format, and they are yours: you can
open them elsewhere, and the app makes no copy of them anywhere else.

Uninstalling the app removes its own data. Files you exported stay where you put them.

## Permissions, and why each exists

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| `VIBRATE` | To play the haptic you are designing on your device's actuator. |

That is the complete list.

## Children

Haptics Workbench is not directed at children and collects no personal information
from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above changes with it, and the
revised policy is published at this same URL.
